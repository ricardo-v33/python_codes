# Theoretical Research
Data analysis and data visualization codes created through the NOIRLab Astro Data Lab JupyterHub environment pertaining to the study of shape and motion of open star clusters. These programs are cross-comparisons of stellar parameter measurements from our own Gaia Data Release 3 datasets to those of *Hunt and Reffer (2023)* (HR2). Pipelines were also created for efficient recalls of calculation and plotting functions as seen in the Exercise/Project notebooks.

`Project_4-Cleaned.ipynb` finds the stars common between our dataset for M67 and the literature. This allows us to observe how the cluster shapes differ in position and velocity space and other stellar parameter space since our cluster was selected by eye and HR2's was selected via a machine learning tool HDBSCAN. `Project_5_Pipelined.ipynb` expands the work of the aforementioned notebook to more clusters and also finds the stars that exist in one dataset's cluster, but not the other. Comprehensive comparisons of these are at the end of the notebook. `shape-motion.ipynb` analyzes the 3-dimensional shape of these clusters in heliocentric position and velocity space, and points out stars that appear to have drastically different parameter values than their counterparts in the same cluster. This leads to a demonstration of how the 3D shape of the cluster elongates for more distant clusters and their fainter stars.

Future work will consist of developing new methodologies to better constrain low-mass star membership of open clusters and observe its effectiveness in maintaining true geometrical representations of the positions and velocities.
